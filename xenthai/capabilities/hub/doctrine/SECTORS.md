# Sector packs

Implements: fuera del playbook (X9)

Which hubs usually come first in a trade, which questions cannot be missing, and which regulatory
topics must be **confirmed with the company**. Read it after `CATALOG.md`, for the company's sector.

The topics are categories, never statements: the detail depends on the country and the legal form.
No law, deadline, amount or authority is written as fact without a source the company provides.

---

## Financial services (credit, factoring, payments, fintech, insurance)
- **First:** Producto · Operaciones · Riesgo y cumplimiento · Ingeniería (if there is an own platform) · IA.
- **Mandatory questions:** what legal form does each entity have · who supervises and which reports are filed · how is the customer known · who approves credit or payments and within which limits · how does money move and who reconciles it.
- **Confirm:** financial supervision, anti-money-laundering, data protection, credit bureaus, cost transparency to the customer, contracts and guarantees.
- **Care:** amounts, rates, customers and portfolio figures never enter a hub.

## Software and SaaS
- **First:** Producto · Ingeniería · Atención a clientes · Comercial · Personas.
- **Mandatory questions:** which plans exist and what each includes · how a change reaches production · what we do in an outage · how support is given and in what times.
- **Confirm:** customer data protection, service-level contracts, security certifications promised.

## Retail and e-commerce
- **First:** Operaciones (orders, inventory, shipping) · Atención a clientes · Comercial or Marketing · Personas.
- **Mandatory questions:** how an order flows · what happens with returns · how inventory is handled · which promotions may be offered.
- **Confirm:** consumer protection, invoicing, personal data, payments.

## Manufacturing and industry
- **First:** Operaciones (production, quality) · Seguridad e higiene · Personas · Compras/Proveedores.
- **Mandatory questions:** the process per line · quality controls · what to do in an accident or a stoppage · how a supplier is onboarded.
- **Confirm:** occupational safety, environmental, product standards, foreign trade.

## Logistics and transport
- **First:** Operaciones · Atención a clientes · Seguridad · Personas.
- **Mandatory questions:** how a route is assigned and followed · what to do in an incident en route · how a delivery is documented.
- **Confirm:** transport permits, insurance, transfer documents, road safety.

## Health
- **First:** clinical or service operations · Riesgo y cumplimiento · Personas · patient care.
- **Mandatory questions:** which protocols apply · how the record is handled · who may see which information.
- **Confirm:** sensitive health data, sanitary licences, informed consent. The area with the most risk of stating something improperly: everything with an owner.

## Professional services and agencies
- **First:** Comercial · Operaciones (project delivery) · Personas · Marca.
- **Mandatory questions:** how a quote is made · how a project starts and closes · how project profitability is measured (no figures).
- **Confirm:** client confidentiality, intellectual property of deliverables.

## Education
- **First:** Personas (teachers) · academic operations · student and family care · IA.
- **Confirm:** minors' data, official validity of studies, school regulations.

## Real estate and construction
- **First:** site or sales operations · Comercial · Riesgo (contracts, permits) · Seguridad.
- **Confirm:** permits and licences, buyer contracts, site safety, anti-money-laundering in sales.

## Restaurants and hospitality
- **First:** operations per station · Personas (high turnover) · quality and hygiene · Atención a clientes.
- **Confirm:** food handling and hygiene, civil protection, labour.

## A sector not listed

Take the closest by business model (who pays, what is delivered, how regulated) and say so in the map.
If `00-PERFIL.md` exists (`company-profile`), its archetype and regulator are the starting point.

## Before you finish

- Every regulatory topic is phrased as something to confirm, with the role who confirms it.
- No law, deadline, amount or authority appears as fact without a company-provided source.
