# Mexican regulators — what can be enforced against a client's public claims

Read this before any public claim is drafted, and during a presence audit. It exists because
**knowing which claims are illegal has to happen before drafting, not at review** — a sentence this
plugin wrote can cost a client a fine, and the fine scales with the client's size.

Every article below was verified against the official text on 2 September 2026, with the reform date
each text carries. Mexican federal law is a public official text, so it may be quoted; anything
**unverified** is marked and must not be presented to a client as established.

---

## 0. Why this scales with company size

**LFPC Art. 128 BIS, second paragraph:** for an Art. 32 breach that is particularly grave, on
**reincidencia**, the fine may reach **up to 10% of the infringer's annual gross revenue from
commercialisation of the goods, products or services featured in the advertising**, for the last
fiscal year in which the infraction was committed.

The same misleading sentence costs a small business a fixed fine and a mid-to-large company a
percentage of a product line's revenue. And **Art. 128 TER** makes "particularly grave" easier to
reach at scale: it includes cases where continued operation could affect the rights and interests of
a **group** of consumers, and cases that could endanger the life, health or safety of a group. A
larger audience makes the group threshold easier to meet, not harder.

**LFPDPPP Art. 60** points the same way from the privacy side: the authority must consider the
responsable's **economic capacity** when setting a sanction.

### And nobody has to complain

PROFECO's own *Guía de Procedimientos de Publicidad Engañosa* (Subprocuraduría de Servicios,
February 2006) states that advertising proceedings **always start *de oficio*, even where a
complaint precedes them**, and that they commonly begin when the substantive area learns of the
advertising *"a través del monitoreo a los medios de comunicación (radio, televisión, impresos y/o
Internet)."*

**PROFECO monitors the internet directly. A client's public social output is a standing,
self-served surveillance surface, and no complainant is required to start a file.**

## 1. PROFECO — misleading and abusive advertising

*Ley Federal de Protección al Consumidor, Última Reforma DOF 12-12-2025.*

### Art. 32 — the core provision, in four operative parts

1. Information or advertising disseminated **by any medium or form** must be *"veraces,
   comprobables, claros y exentos de textos, diálogos, sonidos, imágenes, marcas, denominaciones de
   origen y otras descripciones que induzcan o puedan inducir a error o confusión por engañosas o
   abusivas."*
2. **Truth is not a defence if the presentation misleads.** Engañosa or abusiva information is that
   which, *"sea o no verdadera,"* induces error or confusion *"por la forma inexacta, falsa,
   exagerada, parcial, artificiosa o tendenciosa en que se presenta."*
3. Comparative advertising, *"sean de una misma marca o de distinta,"* may not be engañosa or
   abusiva under that same standard.
4. **Added DOF 11-01-2018 — the highest-yield audit item in the whole law.** It is prohibited to
   state that a product or service has been *"avalados, aprobados, recomendados o certificados por
   sociedades o asociaciones profesionales"* where those bodies lack the documentation supporting,
   with **scientific, objective and reliable evidence**, the qualities or properties claimed.

That fourth part is why `PROOF.md` exists. Every certification, endorsement, award and
professional-body seal in a client's public material needs its supporting document named — or it
comes down.

### The rest of the operative set

| Article | What it requires |
| --- | --- |
| **Art. 1 fracc. VII** | Protection against misleading and abusive advertising is a basic principle of the Law |
| **Art. 34** | Product data, labels, packaging **and the respective advertising** must be in **Spanish**, price in national currency, in comprehensible and legible terms |
| **Art. 35** | PROFECO may order the advertising **suspended** — *"y, en su caso, al medio que la difunda"* — order it **corrected**, and impose sanctions |
| **Art. 25 BIS fracc. VI** | Precautionary suspension where a **collectivity** of consumers is or may be affected in life, health, security or economy |
| **Art. 76 BIS** | Electronic transactions: fracc. IV bars deceptive practices on product characteristics; fracc. VII requires clear and sufficient information, *"en especial tratándose de prácticas de mercadotecnia dirigidas a la población vulnerable, como los niños, ancianos y enfermos"* |
| **Art. 76 BIS fracc. VIII–IX** | **New, DOF 12-12-2025.** Clear, prominent, accessible disclosure of **recurring automatic charges** — periodicity, amount, billing date — express informed consent, at least **five natural days'** notice before automatic renewal with penalty-free cancellation, and a mechanism for **immediate** cancellation. **Any social-driven subscription funnel is now squarely in scope** |

### Sanctions, 2026 amounts

| **Art. 127** | Includes Arts. 33, **34**, **35** | $760.89 – $2,434,847.42 |

### The regulator's own reading rules

*ACUERDO por el que se establecen los Lineamientos para el Análisis y Verificación de la Información
y Publicidad, DOF 24 July 2012.*

- **QUINTO — the jurisdiction carve-out that decides who regulates a client.** PROFECO may analyse
  advertising for any goods or services **except** services rendered under an employment
  relationship, non-commercial professional services, credit-information society services, and
  *"servicios regulados por las leyes financieras"* provided by the institutions in LFPC Art. 5.
  **For a bank, insurer or Sofom, the advertising regulator is CONDUSEF, not PROFECO** — but see §4.
- **SÉPTIMO — the whole-advertisement rule.** PROFECO *"analizará todo el contenido del anuncio
  publicitario, sin descomponer sus partes integrantes,"* including words, numbers, spoken and
  written, visual and musical presentations and sound effects — weighing the nature of the product,
  **the medium**, the audience, and the temporal context. **A disclaimer in the caption does not
  cure a misleading image.**
- **OCTAVO** — where restrictions or conditions are not stated in offer or promotion advertising,
  the supplier cannot later deny the product on the ground that the consumer does not qualify.
- **NOVENO — the burden sits with the advertiser, on demand.** PROFECO may require the supplier to
  produce the documentation substantiating the characteristics attributed to advertised products.
- **DÉCIMO** — PROFECO verifies that stated effects and benefits are **comprobables**; that claims
  of benefits within a stated period have **technical or scientific support**; that **categorical or
  superlative** terms that mislead are avoided; that the advertising does not **discredit** another
  company's establishment, products, activity, services or distinctive signs through false
  assertions; and that comparisons are objective and comprobable.
- **DÉCIMO PRIMERO** — must not mislead as to characteristics, composition, risks or handling; and
  two specifics worth memorising: *"En caso de que el proveedor incluya el precio en su publicidad,
  se deberá verificar que se incluya el precio total final"*, and where the words *"garantizado"*,
  *"garantía"* or an equivalent are used, the advertising must state what the warranty consists of,
  its period of validity, and how the consumer can invoke it.
- **DÉCIMO SEGUNDO / TERCERO** — heightened scrutiny for advertising to vulnerable populations,
  accounting for inexperience, credulity, and psychological or emotional condition.
- **DÉCIMO SEXTO** — **any person** may denounce a practice they consider engañosa or abusiva.

### The two consequences that hurt more than the fine

- **The Art. 32 + Art. 7 BIS criterion.** PROFECO's Dirección de Publicidad y Normas established
  that failing to declare the **total amount payable** is itself the administrative infraction of
  publicidad engañosa. The worked example was airfares advertised at a price with a footnote
  excluding taxes and charges. **Every "desde $X" post without the all-in figure sits under this
  criterion.**
- **The corrective legend.** Since the DOF 04-02-2004 reforms, where the veracity of advertising is
  in doubt PROFECO may order the advertiser to inform consumers with the legend: **"LA VERACIDAD DE
  LA INFORMACIÓN CONTENIDA EN ESTE MENSAJE NO HA SIDO COMPROBADA ANTE PROFECO."** The reputational
  cost of carrying that sentence is far above the fine.

### PROFECO's Decálogo de Publicidad — the regulator's own list of the ten most frequent violations

Use it as the audit checklist. It is not a consultant's opinion; it is the enforcement body's own
enumeration.

1. Testimonials from celebrities, artists or the public where the assertions are not substantiated
   in real, verifiable situations or facts.
2. Using notaries public to lend apparent evidentiary validity to mere attestation of facts that
   require specialised scientific or technical proof.
3. Recommendations from groups, associations, colegios or authorities that do not legally exist, or
   that lack knowledge of the subject matter, or where the endorsement was given in a context of
   partiality or confusion.
4. Attributing extraordinary effects, curative properties or surprising results without duly
   substantiating them in scientific and technical studies.
5. Not holding the competent authority's permit, aviso or registry where the law requires it —
   Salud, Agricultura, Turismo, Educación.
6. Failing to provide necessary information on use, and contraindications, precautions or
   restrictions.
7. Using **"APLICAN RESTRICCIONES"** without specifying what they consist of.
8. Offering results without specifying person-to-person variation.
9. **Failing to display, by any medium, in a manifest and evident manner beyond doubt, the total
   amount the consumer will pay** — price plus taxes, plus interest where applicable, plus shipping
   or any other delivery charge.
10. Legends or purported permits that are illegible or too small to be appreciated at a glance.

### The influencer guide is not binding — and that matters both ways

PROFECO published a *Guía de Publicidad para Influencers* in the DOF on **21 August 2023**, requiring
visible, persistent paid-partnership labelling — `#Publicidad`, `#PublicidadPagada`, `#Patrocinio`
and equivalents — legible throughout video playback, plus disclosure of genuine experience.

**It is not enforceable on its own.** A guide requires a law, regulation or NOM behind it; PROFECO's
then-head merely invited compliance. **The enforceable obligation is Art. 32.** Frame it correctly:
the guide tells you *how PROFECO will read* Art. 32, and Art. 32 is what carries the fine.

## 2. IMPI — comparative advertising has two regulators, and this is the one people miss

*Ley Federal de Protección a la Propiedad Industrial, Última Reforma DOF 03-04-2026.*

**Art. 386** administrative infractions:

- **fracc. I** — acts contrary to good usage and custom in industry, commerce and services implying
  **unfair competition**.
- **fracc. II** — inducing confusion, error or deceit by unfoundedly suggesting a relationship or
  association with a third party's establishment, that products are made under a third party's
  licence or specifications, a false geographic origin, or an **official sponsorship relationship**
  with a mass-attendance event.
- **fracc. III — the operative one.** *"Intentar o lograr el propósito de desprestigiar los
  productos, los servicios, la actividad industrial o comercial o el establecimiento de otro. **No
  estará comprendida en esta disposición, la comparación de productos o servicios que ampare la
  marca con el propósito de informar al público, siempre que dicha comparación no sea tendenciosa,
  falsa o exagerada en los términos de la Ley Federal de Protección al Consumidor.**"*
- **fracc. XVI** — indicating that goods or services are protected by a registered trademark when
  they are not. **This includes a stale ® on a profile after a registration lapsed or was
  cancelled**, from one year after lapse.
- **fracc. XVIII and XX** — using a registered mark or a confusingly similar sign as an element of a
  **domain name**, trade name or corporate name without written consent. **This is the leverage for
  recovering a handle held by a former agency, employee or squatter.**

**Read the fracc. III safe harbour carefully.** Comparative advertising is lawful in Mexico, but its
lawfulness is **defined by reference to the LFPC standard.** So a competitor comparison must satisfy
*both* LFPC Art. 32 and the Lineamientos' DÉCIMO fracc. V–VI, *and* survive LFPPI Art. 386 fracc.
III. A competitor brings the IMPI action; PROFECO can act *de oficio* on the same post.

**Art. 388 sanctions:** fine up to **250,000 UMA per conduct**, an additional fine up to **1,000 UMA
per day** the infraction persists, temporary closure up to 90 days, or definitive closure.

**New, and current:** the DOF 03-04-2026 reform added a final paragraph to Art. 386 —
*"Las conductas previstas en las fracciones anteriores también serán sancionadas si se realizan
mediante el uso de Inteligencia Artificial."*

## 3. COFEPRIS — health, food and beverage claims

*Ley General de Salud, Título Decimotercero; Reglamento de la Ley General de Salud en Materia de
Publicidad, DOF 04-05-2000, Última Reforma DOF 08-09-2022.*

### Statutory base

| Article | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **LGS Art. 300** | Advertising relating to health, disease treatment, rehabilitation, the exercise of health disciplines, and the products and services the Law covers requires the Secretaría's **authorisation** |
| **LGS Art. 301** | Advertising promoting use, sale or consumption **directly or indirectly** of insumos para la salud, alcoholic beverages and regulated products is subject to authorisation |
| **LGS Art. 305** | *"Los responsables de la publicidad, anunciantes, agencias de publicidad y medios difusores, se ajustarán a las normas de este título."* **Liability reaches the advertiser, the agency AND the medium** |
| **LGS Art. 306** | Information on quality, origin, purity, conservation, nutritional properties and benefits **must be comprobable**; the message must correspond to the sanitary authorisation granted |
| **LGS Art. 307** | Food and non-alcoholic beverage advertising must not attribute to industrialised foods *"un valor superior o distinto al que tengan en realidad"* |

### What is prohibited outright

- **RLGSMP Art. 6 fracc. I — the central prohibition for foods and supplements.** Advertising must
  not *"Atribuirles cualidades preventivas, terapéuticas, rehabilitatorias, nutritivas, estimulantes
  o de otra índole, que no correspondan a su función o uso."*
- **RLGSMP Art. 6 fracc. I Bis** — no seals or legends of recommendation or recognition by
  professional organisations for foods and non-alcoholic beverages that must carry front-of-pack
  excess-nutrient warning seals. **Stricter than LFPC Art. 32 for sealed products.**
- **RLGSMP Art. 9** — advertising is deemed **not comprobable** where it induces error, conceals
  necessary contraindications, **exaggerates** characteristics or properties, suggests the product
  determines physical, intellectual or sexual characteristics without conclusive proof, or compares
  products with different ingredients where that could generate health risk.
- **RLGSMP Art. 21** — foods, supplements and non-alcoholic beverages must not be presented as
  **stimulants or modifiers of a person's physical or mental state**, except as recognised by the
  Secretaría.
- **RLGSMP Art. 24 Bis** — advertising of pre-packaged foods and beverages carrying a front-of-pack
  warning element **must not include** children's characters, animations, cartoons, celebrities,
  athletes or mascots, nor interactive elements, directed at children.
- **RLGSMP Art. 28** — **supplement advertising must carry the legend the Secretaría determines in
  the advertising authorisation**, based on the product's health risk.

### Social media is expressly inside the permit regime

**RLGSMP Art. 22 Bis:** advertising on **open television, restricted television, cinemas, internet
and other digital platforms** promoting foods and non-alcoholic beverages **requires a COFEPRIS
permit where the product's label carries the front-of-pack labelling system.** Resolution period up
to 20 business days.

**RLGSMP Art. 79 requires a PERMISO** for advertising relating to: health services (except
individually rendered); **suplementos alimenticios** and biotechnological products; alcoholic
beverages and tobacco; medicines and herbal remedies; medical equipment, prostheses, diagnostic
agents, dental supplies, surgical and dressing materials and hygienic products; **beauty and
aesthetic services and procedures**; pesticides; toxic plant nutrients; toxic or hazardous
substances; and foods and non-alcoholic beverages on television, cinema, **internet and other
digital platforms** that do not conform to the Art. 22 Bis lineamientos.

**RLGSMP Art. 86 requires an AVISO** for the professional, technical and auxiliary health activities
under LGS Título Cuarto Capítulo I, and for insumos para la salud directed at health professionals.
**Art. 87:** the aviso must be filed within **five days after dissemination begins**.

### The two highest-yield audit items

- **RLGSMP Art. 19 — for any clinic, hospital, dental, aesthetic or wellness client.** Those
  practising the health activities under LGS Título Cuarto Capítulo I *"deberán expresar en la
  publicidad que realicen al respecto, **cualquiera que sea el medio publicitario de que se
  trate**, la institución educativa que les expidió el título, diploma o certificado
  correspondiente y, en su caso, el número de cédula profesional."* **This is almost universally
  absent from Mexican clinic Instagram and Facebook content, and "cualquiera que sea el medio"
  leaves no social exemption.**
- **The permit-versus-published mismatch.** **RLGSMP Art. 6 fracc. III** requires the message's
  elements to correspond to the sanitary authorisation, and **Art. 83** prohibits the permit holder
  from introducing **any** modification into authorised advertising. Companies routinely obtain a
  permit for a television or print execution and then publish unapproved variants on social. **Ask
  for the permit number and compare it against what is published** — the defect is invisible
  internally because regulatory and marketing never compare the two.

**RLGSMP Art. 18** — health-service advertising **will not be authorised** where it offers
preventive, curative or rehabilitative treatments *"cuya eficacia no haya sido comprobada
científicamente."*

**Unverified:** the LGS Título Decimoctavo sanction amounts for advertising violations were not
extracted. **Do not state a COFEPRIS fine figure without checking.**

## 4. CONDUSEF, CNBV — financial product advertising

### Who regulates whom

PROFECO's Lineamientos (QUINTO) **exclude** services regulated by the financial laws and provided by
the institutions in LFPC Art. 5 — for those, the advertising regulator is **CONDUSEF**. But
**LTOSF Art. 12 ¶2** gives **PROFECO** the parallel power over *Entidades Comerciales* for credit,
loans, financing and payment means they issue. **A retailer's own-brand credit card is PROFECO's,
not CONDUSEF's.**

### Ley de Protección y Defensa al Usuario de Servicios Financieros
*Última Reforma DOF 14-11-2025.*

- **Art. 11 fracc. XV** — CONDUSEF may *"ordenar la suspensión de la información que induzca a
  error"* directed at Users about financial products and services.
- **Art. 8 — the REUS, and co-responsibility.** Financial institutions are **prohibited** from using
  their client database for marketing or advertising purposes, and from sending advertising to
  clients who declined it or are on the register. And: *"Las Instituciones Financieras que sean
  objeto de publicidad son **corresponsables** del manejo de la información de sus Clientes cuando
  dicha publicidad la envíen a través de terceros."* **The institution is co-responsible for its
  agency's or lead-generation partner's conduct.**
- **Art. 94 sanctions:** fracc. XIV(a) — **500 to 2,000 días de salario** for failing to comply with
  a suspension order. Fracc. XII — **250 to 2,000 días de salario** for sending advertising to Users
  who asked not to be disturbed at home, workplace, *"dirección electrónica o por cualquier otro
  medio"*, or who are on the REUS. Fracc. XVII — 500 to 2,000 for departing from sound practices.
  And: *"Las Instituciones Financieras que sean objeto de publicidad serán acreedoras a la misma
  sanción."* Reincidence up to double.

### Ley para la Transparencia y Ordenamiento de los Servicios Financieros
*Última Reforma DOF 14-11-2025.*

- **Art. 12** — CONDUSEF's general dispositions must consider veracity and precision, absence of
  unfair competition, transparency of characteristics and **inherent risks**, and mechanisms for
  making commissions publicly known. CONDUSEF may suspend advertising implying inexactitud, unfair
  competition, or anything that could induce error.
- **Art. 15 — the item that fails in character-limited formats.** For credits, loans or financing to
  which the **CAT** applies, *"la publicidad y los Contratos de Adhesión deberán contener dicho
  CAT"*, and *"deberá **resaltarse en caracteres distintivos de manera clara, notoria e
  indubitable**."* **Art. 15 Bis** imposes the same for the **GAT** on deposit-taking.
- **Sanctions:** **Art. 42 fracc. IV** — **2,000 to 5,000 días de salario** for advertising that
  breaches Art. 12. **Art. 43 fracc. V and VII** — **4,000 to 150,000 UMA** for failing to suspend,
  or for not incorporating the CAT or not highlighting it.

**Audit item:** any Instagram or Facebook creative advertising a credit product without the CAT in
distinctive characters. It fails in social formats specifically, and it is invisible internally
because the compliance sign-off covered the brochure, not the post.

### Ley Fintech
*Última Reforma DOF 14-11-2025.*

- **Art. 11 final paragraph** — ITFs must state that neither the Federal Government nor paraestatal
  entities guarantee clients' resources, *"en sus respectivas páginas de internet, en los mensajes
  que muestren a través de las aplicaciones informáticas o transmitan por medios de comunicación
  electrónica o digital"*, and in advertising and contracts. They must also take measures to prevent
  false or misleading information being disseminated through them.
- **Art. 12** — an authorised ITF must *"difundir de manera notoria a través de los medios por los
  cuales contacten a sus Clientes, que se encuentran autorizadas, reguladas y supervisadas por las
  Autoridades Financieras."* The reserved expressions may not be used in any name, corporate name or
  **publicidad**, nor in interfaces, apps, internet pages *"o cualquier otro medio de comunicación
  electrónica o digital"*, by anyone unauthorised.
- **Art. 103 fracc. I** — CNBV fine of **1,000 to 5,000 UMA** on unauthorised persons using those
  reserved terms in their name, advertising, interfaces or internet pages.

**Unverified:** **LIC Art. 103** (captación irregular) is repeatedly cited as the basis for CNBV's
public alerts against unauthorised entities using *"publicidad masiva (en redes sociales, páginas
web, correos electrónicos)"*. **The article number was not verified — check it before citing.** CNBV
does publish a standing list of entities not authorised to take resources, which is a real,
checkable artefact for any client whose social content promises returns.

## 5. Data protection — the law changed in 2025, and most Mexican privacy notices are now wrong

*Ley Federal de Protección de Datos Personales en Posesión de los Particulares, published DOF
20 March 2025, in force 21 March 2025; Última Reforma DOF 14-11-2025. **This is a NEW law that
replaced the 2010 LFPDPPP.***

### The enforcement body changed

**Art. 2 fracc. XV** defines "Secretaría" as the **Secretaría Anticorrupción y Buen Gobierno**. INAI
was extinguished by the December 2024 constitutional reform, and **Art. 59** provides that
infractions *"serán sancionadas por la Secretaría."*

**A privacy notice naming INAI as the authority, or citing the 2010 law, is out of date on its
face.** This is a thirty-second check in a first session, it dates the client's last compliance
review to before March 2025, and it is a proxy for the whole governance stack.

### Art. 15 — mandatory minimum content of a privacy notice

| # | Requirement |
| --- | --- |
| I | The responsable's identity and domicile |
| II | The personal data to be processed, **identifying which are sensitive** |
| III | The purposes of processing, **distinguishing those requiring consent** |
| IV | The options and means offered to limit use or disclosure |
| V | The mechanisms, means and procedures for exercising **ARCO** rights |
| VI | The procedure and means by which changes to the notice will be communicated |

Two deltas against the old law: the express obligation to state **transfers** has been removed, and
**distinguishing consent-requiring purposes is new.**

### Art. 16 fracc. II — the provision that catches every social funnel

*"Cuando los datos personales sean obtenidos por cualquier **medio electrónico, óptico, sonoro,
visual, o a través de cualquier otra tecnología**, deberá ser proporcionado en su modalidad
**simplificada** la que deberá contener al menos la información a que se refieren las fracciones I a
IV del artículo anterior, y señalar el sitio donde se podrá consultar el aviso de privacidad
integral."*

Applied to social channels, **each of these requires a simplified privacy notice at the point of
collection**, with Art. 15 fracc. I–IV plus a pointer to the integral notice:

- An Instagram or WhatsApp DM asking for name, phone and requirement
- A Meta Lead Ad form
- A link-in-bio form
- A Messenger chatbot
- A comment inviting *"manda DM con tus datos"*
- **A raffle or giveaway collecting entrant data**

In practice it is almost never present, because the notice was written for the website and nobody
owns the social funnel.

### Security and breach

- **Art. 18** — the responsable may not adopt security measures **lower than those it maintains for
  its own information.** Shared social-account credentials on an inbox receiving personal data are
  a measure below the organisation's own standard.
- **Art. 19** — breaches significantly affecting patrimonial or moral rights must be reported to
  data subjects **immediately**. A compromised brand account with a DM inbox is a breach vector.

### Sanctions

- **Art. 58 fracc. V** — omitting any or all of the Art. 15 elements is an infraction.
- **Art. 59 fracc. II** — **100 to 160,000 UMA** for Art. 58 fracc. II–VII, which includes the
  privacy-notice omission. **Fracc. III** — 200 to 320,000 UMA for fracc. VIII–XVIII. **Fracc. IV** —
  an additional 100 to 320,000 UMA where infractions persist, and **sanctions may be doubled where
  sensitive data are involved.**
- **Art. 60** — the authority must weigh the nature of the data, intentionality, the responsable's
  **economic capacity**, and reincidence.
- **Art. 62** — **three months to three years' imprisonment** for a person authorised to process
  personal data who, for profit, causes a security breach of the databases in their custody.

**Unverified:** whether the *Lineamientos del Aviso de Privacidad* (DOF 17 January 2013) and the old
Reglamento survive the 2025 law. Art. 17 references *"el Reglamento de esta Ley."* **Check the
transitional articles of the 20-03-2025 decree before advising on notice format.**

## 6. CONAR — self-regulation, and why a competitor uses it first

CONAR (Consejo de Autorregulación y Ética Publicitaria) was constituted as a civil organisation in
**1996**, composed of advertisers, agencies, media, chambers and associations; in 2013 it had 90
members. Its first work concerned **comparative advertising** disputes. Its Consejo Directivo *"puede
aplicar la suspensión provisional de mensajes, mientras resuelve."*

*(Source: García-Calderón, Carola (2017), "Ética publicitaria en México," El profesional de la
información 26(2), 311–319 — peer-reviewed, UNAM. The article also notes the critique that CONAR
"excluye cualquier participación gubernamental y de la sociedad.")*

**Why it matters operationally:** CONAR resolutions are not state sanctions, but **a competitor can
obtain provisional suspension through CONAR faster than a PROFECO or IMPI proceeding would
resolve.** For a client belonging to an industry chamber it is also a membership-facing obligation.
The **Código PABI** covers food and non-alcoholic beverage advertising to children (2009); **COSMEP**
covers cosmetics.

**Unverified:** the current text and article numbers of CONAR's Código de Ética Publicitaria —
conar.org.mx blocked retrieval. Secondary reporting describes seven principles including truthful
and honest information, fair competition and objective comparisons. **Confirm against the code
before citing article numbers.**

## 7. How to use this in a session

1. **`BRAND.md` records the regulator.** Which of these applies is a company-level fact captured
   once, not re-derived per piece. A client with a sealed food product, a clinic, or a credit product
   has a different legal floor, and finding that out at review is too late.
2. **Every claim that touches this doctrine gets a `PROOF.md` row** naming its supporting document
   and its re-verification date. Art. 32's fourth part and the Lineamientos' NOVENO both put the
   burden of substantiation on the advertiser, on demand.
3. **Screen before drafting, not at review.** A prohibited claim rewritten is cheap; a published one
   is a file opened *de oficio*.
4. **State the total price or do not state a price.** Nine of PROFECO's own ten most frequent
   violations are avoidable by rule; this is the one that appears most in social formats.
5. **Never advise on a specific case from this document alone.** It is a screening instrument that
   says *this needs a lawyer* — it is not legal advice, and this practice does not give legal
   advice. Say that to the client in those words.
